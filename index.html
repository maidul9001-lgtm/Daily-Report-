about

Built on a touchscreen, not a desk

I'm a self-taught developer from Bangladesh who builds software entirely from an Android phone. No laptop, no desktop — just Termux, a lot of patience, and a habit of shipping things that work.

I work across HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Python — building web apps, small tools and automation scripts — and I also take on data entry and structured-data work for clients who need accuracy over anything else.

Every project below, including this site, was written, tested and deployed from a phone screen. If that sounds like a limitation, I'd argue it's made me better at writing lean, focused code.
